PATTERN HUNTER

Study the clues. Crack the hidden rule. Race to the centre.
How to play

Every square has two distances, a and b, to its nearest edges, shown as a·b. One hidden maths rule about a and b decides which squares are traps, and it is the same in all four corners. Red squares are known traps, and green squares are known safe. The number on a green square counts the traps on its four sides (up, down, left and right), not the diagonals. Players take turns, one step each, with 20 seconds per turn. Stepping on a trap bounces you back and you miss your next turn, and a second trap in a row sends you home. When you think you know the rule, press Declare. Each player gets only one guess per game, and once someone guesses right, no more guesses are allowed. A right answer makes you immune to traps for 3 steps but reveals nothing to anyone else. A wrong answer costs you two turns. First to the gold centre wins.
